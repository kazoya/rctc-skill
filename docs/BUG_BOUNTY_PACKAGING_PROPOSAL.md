# Bug Bounty Training — Free / Pro packaging proposal

Does **not** change the MIT license of the open RCTC core.

## Portland–Pozzolanic blend (packaging metaphor)
| Layer | Role | Analogy |
|---|---|---|
| **Portland (MIT Free)** | Structural binder — ethics, scope guard, safe labs, report outline, validator | Cement clinker |
| **Pozzolan (Pro / Coffee)** | Reactive polish — mentor checklists, annotated reports, expanded lab packs | Pozzolanic strength gain |
| **Cure time** | Learner practice in local labs | Strength develops with use |

No DRM. No invented payment checkout URLs beyond the public support link.

## Suggested split
| Tier | Who | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Free (MIT)** | Everyone | Modules 00–05 (EN+AR), Scope Guard, localhost/synthetic examples, validator, SAFETY_MODEL |
| **Pro (Coffee Pass)** | Supporters who bought a coffee and opened a [Coffee Pass](https://github.com/kazoya/rctc-skill/issues/new?template=coffee_pass.yml) issue | `tracks/bug-bounty/pro/` mentor checklists, annotated report samples, expanded lab narrative packs |
