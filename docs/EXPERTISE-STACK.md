## Marketing position

**RCTC is an evidence-first skill operating system: it turns vague goals into safe, composable, verifiable delivery while preserving human authority.**

HTPAAP and WEHM add the missing accumulated-expertise layer:
- HTPAAP asks what excellent engineers actually prove through architecture, tests, benchmarks, issues, and compatibility promises.
- WEHM asks whether testing is authorized, proportionate, evidenced, remediable, and responsibly disclosed.
