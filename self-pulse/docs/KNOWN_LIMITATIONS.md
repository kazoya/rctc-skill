# Known Limitations & Customer Support Requirements

## Product limitations

1. Free edition state is **in-process** — lost on restart unless the host persists JSON.
2. Outcomes must be **externally verified**; the model must not grade itself.
3. `FunctionalSatisfactionScore` is **computational**, not an emotion.
4. CSAT is **never inferred**; only explicit customer text is stored.
5. Premium CRM adapters are **stubs** until credentials are owner-configured.
6. Not a full legal/compliance suite (consent UX, DPA, encryption-at-rest must be host-provided).
7. Project SELF scientific pilots are **independent** — do not market Self-Pulse as proof of consciousness research success.
